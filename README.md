# Bulk Review Manager

An Anki add-on for auditing and refreshing note collections where the content can go stale.

## Why use it?

Some subjects change over time. Medicine, law, software, current affairs, language usage, and product details all shift. Your cards can quietly become outdated without you noticing. Bulk Review Manager gives you a fast slideshow to review, edit, and triage those cards in bulk, outside the normal study queue.

## Features

- Filter notes by deck, card status (New, Learning, Review, Relearning, Suspended), date added, or date last edited.
- Add any custom Anki search on top of the filters.
- Review selected notes one by one in a dedicated slideshow editor.
- Approve a card to save your edits and automatically unsuspend it.
- Reject a card to suspend it without saving changes.
- See status, date added, and last edited info for each card at a glance.
- Unsaved change warning when closing the review window.

## Great for

- Refreshing factual cards in fast moving subjects.
- Cleaning up imported decks.
- Batch editing vocabulary or definitions.
- Triaging large note collections before studying.

## Installation

1. Open Anki.
2. Go to **Tools > Add-ons > Get Add-ons**.
3. Paste the add-on code and click OK.
4. Restart Anki.
5. Open **Tools > Bulk Review** from the menu bar.

## Usage

1. Open **Tools > Bulk Review**.
2. Pick a deck, or leave it on "(All decks)".
3. Optionally enable **Filter by date** and choose added or edited.
4. Optionally choose a **Status** filter.
5. Add any extra search terms if you want.
6. Click **Search**.
7. Check the notes you want to review.
8. Click **Start Review**.
9. In the slideshow, use **Approve** to save and unsuspend, or **Reject** to suspend without saving.
10. Use **Previous** and **Next** to move through the cards.

## Requirements

- Anki 26.09.3 or later.
